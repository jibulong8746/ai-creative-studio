Privacy Policy

AI Creative Studio

Last updated: October 24, 2026

AI Creative Studio is a locally operated video creation and content management application. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when the application is used and when supported third-party platform integrations are enabled.

1. Information We Process

The application may process video files, audio files, titles, descriptions, publishing preferences, and related project information provided by its users.

When a user chooses to connect a supported social media account, the application may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, and information necessary to perform the requested integration, subject to the permissions granted by the user.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to create and manage videos, prepare publishing content, authenticate connected accounts, and perform user-authorized publishing actions where the relevant platform permissions are available.

3. TikTok Integration

TikTok integration is a planned feature and is not represented as an approved or operational publishing integration unless and until the required permissions have been granted and implemented.

If enabled, users will authorize access through TikTok's official authorization process. The application will request only the permissions needed for supported features. Publishing actions will be initiated by the authorized user.

4. Data Storage and Security

Project files and application records are primarily managed in the local operating environment. Any credentials or authorization tokens used for integrations should be protected against unauthorized access. No system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Information Sharing

Content and information may be transmitted to third-party services when a user explicitly requests a feature that depends on those services. Such services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored project information may be retained until removed by the application operator. Users may revoke third-party account authorization through the applicable platform's account settings. Revoking authorization does not necessarily remove content already published on a third-party platform.

7. Third-Party Services

Where enabled, AI Creative Studio may interact with external services for content generation, account authorization, and publishing. Each external service may process information according to its own privacy policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality, integrations, or data handling practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.
